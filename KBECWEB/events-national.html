ReinforCE: Where Civil Minds Connect & Innovate

The premier annual national civil engineering championship organized by the ACI Student Chapter - KUET, Department of Civil Engineering. Fostering competitive engineering brilliance, structural mechanics, and academic networking across Bangladesh.

National Flagship 1.0 • 18 January 2025

ReinforCE 1.0 Events & Segments

Held on 18 January 2025 at KUET Civil Engineering Department. Nine signature segments brought together top engineering talents from all over the country. Official rulebooks and registration records below:

Truss Trim

Segment 01

Design, fabricate, and test lightweight balsa wood truss bridge models. Bridges are subjected to destructive central point loading on the KUET hydraulic testing floor, evaluated strictly on load-to-weight structural efficiency.

Team Size: 2 – 4 Members
Venue: KUET Structures & Concrete Lab Wing

Crack The Case

Segment 02

The premier civil-business case solving competition. Multidisciplinary teams formulate engineering, financial feasibility, and logistical solutions for real-world mega-infrastructure and coastal defense challenges in Bangladesh.

Team Size: 3 – 4 Members
Venue: KUET CE Seminar Hall & Auditorium

Poster Presentation

Segment 03

Academic research and innovation showcase. Undergraduate scholars display novel studies in concrete materials science, eco-friendly supplementary cementitious materials (SCMs), geotechnical stability, and earthquake resilience.

Team Size: 1 – 3 Members
Venue: Central Exhibition Gallery, Dept. of Civil Engineering

Line of Vision

Segment 04

Field surveying and geomatics alignment showdown. Teams demonstrate speed and millimeter-level accuracy utilizing optical levels and total stations across complex campus benchmarks and elevation traverses.

Team Size: 3 Members
Venue: KUET Central Field & Campus Survey Circuit

Mechanics Mania

Segment 05

The ultimate engineering mechanics and statics olympiad. Rapid-fire problem solving testing free-body diagrams (FBDs), Mohr's circle stress transformations, shear-moment kinetics (SFD/BMD), and elastic deflection.

Category: Individual Contest
Format: Written Preliminary + Live Stage Buzzer Round

CAD Buster

Segment 06

High-intensity computer-aided drafting and structural detailing challenge. Draft complex reinforced concrete sections and foundation plans in AutoCAD under strict ACI 318 and BNBC 2020 drafting codification.

Category: Individual Contest
Venue: Departmental Computer & CAD Workstation Labs

Motion Craft

Segment 07

3D architectural animation, structural walkthroughs, and 4D construction sequencing. Participants render dynamic visual narratives portraying how modern concrete structures are conceived, built, and integrated.

Team Size: 1 – 2 Members
Submission: High-Definition Rendered Video Showcase

Technical Writing

Segment 08

Scientific writing and technical journalism competition. Critical analysis of emerging structural engineering topics, smart sensor embedded concrete, climate-resilient bridges, and infrastructure asset management.

Category: Individual Contest
Format: Structured Technical Paper & Defense

Photo Quest

Segment 09

Civil engineering and architectural photography contest. Capturing the grandeur of monumental construction, raw concrete textures, cantilever geometry, and infrastructure workers reshaping the national skyline.

Category: Open Submission
Exhibition: KUET Central Fest Gallery

ACI KUET Student Chapter

Rooted in the Department of Civil Engineering at KUET and chartered by the American Concrete Institute (ACI), the ACI KUET Student Chapter is a dynamic platform for aspiring civil and materials engineers. We strive to cultivate hands-on innovation, sustainable construction practices, and leadership, connecting KUET students with nationwide industry networks and global engineering forums.
