Chapter Overview & Mission

The official student chapter of the American Concrete Institute at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), dedicated to advancing concrete engineering and student leadership.

Our Heritage & Foundation

Building the Premier ACI Student Chapter at KUET

The American Concrete Institute (ACI) Student Chapter at KUET is the premier departmental student initiative established under the Department of Civil Engineering, Khulna University of Engineering & Technology.

Conceived and spearheaded by founding convener Shakil Ilhan (Roll: 2301044) under the academic mentorship of Senior Professor Dr. Abu Zakir Morshed, the chapter acts as a vital bridge between rigorous departmental coursework and international concrete construction standards established by ACI (Farmington Hills, Michigan, USA).

Chapter Charter Facts

Affiliation: American Concrete Institute (ACI International, USA)
Parent Chapter: ACI Bangladesh Chapter
Academic Base: Dept. of Civil Engineering, KUET, Khulna-9203
Faculty Moderator: Prof. Dr. Abu Zakir Morshed
Initiator & Convener: Shakil Ilhan (Roll: 2301044)
Official Flagship Fest: ReinforCE National Concrete Fest
Web Domain: acikuet.org

Our Mission

To empower undergraduate civil engineering students of KUET with hands-on concrete testing competencies, structural design software proficiency, research opportunities, and leadership experience that conform to international ACI standards and real-world construction demands.

Our Vision

To establish KUET as the preeminent collegiate center for advanced concrete science, sustainable low-carbon cementitious research, and competitive engineering innovation in South Asia, recognized annually as an ACI Outstanding / Excellent Student Chapter.

Strategic Pillars of Excellence

The core foundations guiding every seminar, competition, and laboratory workshop at ACI KUET

1. Academic Rigor

Mastery of ACI 318-19, ACI 211.1-91, and BNBC 2020 building code provisions, accompanied by software bootcamps in ETABS, SAP2000, and SAFE.

2. Experimental Practice

Direct laboratory batching, slump verification, non-destructive testing (UPV), and 2000 kN digital UTM crushing trials in the KUET concrete lab.

3. Competitive Dominance

Organizing the premier national engineering fest ReinforCE and representing KUET in ACI International competitions with travel grant ambitions.

4. Industry Bridge

Forging strategic linkages with national cement leaders (Akij, Bashundhara, Holcim) and global alumni practicing in top engineering consultancies.

Democratic Student Governance

Structured 4-Tier Leadership Pipeline

A structured succession model guaranteeing institutional continuity across every undergraduate cohort of Civil Engineering, KUET.

Apex Advisory Tier

Faculty Advisor & Moderator (Prof. Dr. Abu Zakir Morshed) providing academic oversight, joint bank signatory verification, and ACI International liaison.

Executive Committee (4th-Year Senior Class)

Chapter President, Vice President (Technical), Vice President (Operations), General Secretary, Joint Secretary, and Treasurer overseeing all strategic operations.

Secretariat & Leads (3rd-Year Junior Class)

Public Relations Secretary, Publications & Editorial Lead, Event Logistics Director, and Technical Research Coordinators.

Sub-Committees & Cohort Delegates (1st & 2nd-Year Undergraduates)

Appointed class representatives, lab testing assistants, competition team apprentices, and web/graphics design volunteers.

ACI KUET Student Chapter

Rooted in the Department of Civil Engineering at KUET and chartered by the American Concrete Institute (ACI), the ACI KUET Student Chapter is a dynamic platform for aspiring civil and materials engineers. We strive to cultivate hands-on innovation, sustainable construction practices, and leadership, connecting KUET students with nationwide industry networks and global engineering forums.
