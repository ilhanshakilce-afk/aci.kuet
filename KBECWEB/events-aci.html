All-Bangladesh ACI Student Chapter Meeting & Rally

Hosted at BUET Civil Engineering Department (Previous Edition Milestone)

Understanding the ACI Student Chapter Meeting:

The National ACI Student Chapters Meeting is not a mere closed administrative committee - it is the flagship annual inter-university convention and competitive rally uniting all recognized American Concrete Institute (ACI) Student Chapters across Bangladesh, including KUET, BUET, CUET, RUET, IUT, AUST, UAP, and SUST.

The previous national edition was proudly hosted at the Department of Civil Engineering, BUET (Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology). The convention featured high-octane engineering rallies: standard concrete cylinder compressive strength showdowns, rapid-fire structural mechanics buzzer battles, innovative pozzolanic binder poster showcases, and collaborative academic networking with visiting American Concrete Institute international leadership.

ACI KUET Student Chapter

Rooted in the Department of Civil Engineering at KUET and chartered by the American Concrete Institute (ACI), the ACI KUET Student Chapter is a dynamic platform for aspiring civil and materials engineers. We strive to cultivate hands-on innovation, sustainable construction practices, and leadership, connecting KUET students with nationwide industry networks and global engineering forums.
