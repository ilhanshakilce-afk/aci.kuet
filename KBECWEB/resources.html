Building Codes & Design Criteria

International Concrete Codes & Specifications Directory

Authoritative technical standards codified by the American Concrete Institute (ACI) and the Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC).

Structural Design Standard

ACI 318-19: Building Code Requirements for Structural Concrete

The definitive global code covering materials, analysis, design of beams, one-way and two-way slabs, columns, shear walls, foundations, and earthquake-resistant detailing provisions (Chapter 18).

  • Chapter 9: Beams & One-Way Flexural Elements
  • Chapter 11: Two-Way Slabs & Punching Shear
  • Chapter 18: Earthquake-Resistant Structural Systems
  • Chapter 22: Sectional Strength & Shear Friction
Access ACI 318-19 Guidelines
Mix Proportioning Guide

ACI 211.1-91: Standard Practice for Selecting Proportions for Concrete

The international benchmark for designing normal, heavyweight, and mass concrete mixes. Detailed step-by-step procedures based on slump, maximum aggregate size, water-cement ratio curves, and coarse aggregate volume.

  • Water-Cement Ratio vs. Target Strength Relationships
  • Aggregate Fineness Modulus (FM) Adjustment Tables
  • Moisture Correction & Absorption Compensation
  • Entrained Air & Chemical Admixture Dosing
Access ACI 211.1 Standard
National Building Code

BNBC 2020: Part 6, Chapter 5 (Concrete Material & Reinforcement)

Bangladesh's gazetted building code regulating reinforced concrete structures across Seismic Zones 1 to 4, coastal salinity exposure classes, minimum cover provisions, and wind load considerations for the Bay of Bengal region.

  • Section 5.3: Minimum Cover for Saline & Coastal Zones
  • Section 5.6: Development Length & Lap Splicing
  • Table 6.5.1: Maximum Water-Cement Ratios
  • Seismic Detailing for Special Moment Resisting Frames (SMRF)
Review BNBC 2020 Provisions

ACI 301-20: Specifications for Concrete Construction

Contractor and engineer specifications regulating formwork design, tolerances, reinforcement placement, batching, mixing, transport, consolidation, finishing, and moist curing protocols.

  • Section 3: Rebar Tolerances & Chair Spacing
  • Section 4: Hot Weather & Cold Weather Placement
  • Section 5: Curing Methods & Minimum Moist Periods

In-depth research guide on steel, synthetic (polypropylene), glass, and natural fibers in concrete matrices. Essential reference for students preparing for the ACI International Concrete Bowling Ball & Egg Protection competitions.

  • Crack Arrest Mechanics & Post-Peak Toughness

  • Impact Resistance & Dynamic Shatter Mitigation
  • Industrial Flooring & Shotcrete Applications

KUET Materials Lab: Concrete Testing & Quality Control Handbook

BNBC 2020: Part 6 Structural Design Compendium (Gazetted)

Civil Engineering Software Learning Roadmaps

Curated step-by-step pathways for students to achieve industry-ready competence in computational structural modeling, finite element analysis, and building information modeling (BIM).

CSI ETABS 21

Zero to Pro • Beginner to Pro
High-Rise Analysis & RCC Design

Primary industry software for 3D modeling of multistorey commercial towers, seismic response spectrum, wind tunnel simulations, and automated reinforcement scheduling.

  • Grid generation & story data assignment
  • Diaphragm constraints & mass center calculation
  • BNBC 2020 Equivalent Static vs Response Spectrum
  • P-Delta non-linear geometric stiffness effects
  • Shear wall design (Pier & Spandrel detailing)
Explore ETABS Roadmap →

CSI SAP2000

Zero to Pro • Beginner to Pro
General FEM, Trusses & Bridges

Finite element powerhouse for arbitrary 2D/3D civil geometries: space trusses, cable-stayed spans, culverts, transmission towers, and industrial precast frames.

  • Joint restraints, springs & support releases
  • Shell element meshing & out-of-plane bending
  • Moving vehicular loads (AASHTO / Roads & Highways)
  • Eigenvalue modal frequency extraction
  • Time-history earthquake ground motion analysis
Explore SAP2000 Roadmap →

CSI SAFE

Zero to Pro • Beginner to Pro
Foundations, Footings & Slabs

Specialized tool for foundation engineering, mat/raft foundations, pile caps, subgrade soil spring stiffness distribution, and punching shear design.

  • Modulus of subgrade reaction (k-value) modeling
  • Isolated, combined & strap footing design
  • Mat foundation with nonlinear tension-free soil
  • Column punching shear critical perimeter check
  • Post-tensioned tendon profiling & stress losses
Explore CSI SAFE Roadmap →

  • Dynamic blocks, layers & standard lineweights
  • Beam longitudinal elevation & section cross-cuts
  • Column ties, crossties & splice confinement zones
  • Foundation pile layout coordinates & grids
  • Automated Bar Bending Schedule (BBS) spreadsheet link

ACI KUET Student Chapter

Rooted in the Department of Civil Engineering at KUET and chartered by the American Concrete Institute (ACI), the ACI KUET Student Chapter is a dynamic platform for aspiring civil and materials engineers. We strive to cultivate hands-on innovation, sustainable construction practices, and leadership, connecting KUET students with nationwide industry networks and global engineering forums.
