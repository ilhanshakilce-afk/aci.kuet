Alumni in Industry Spotlight

Connecting undergraduate fellows with KUET Civil Engineering graduates excelling as structural design directors, project managers, and doctoral researchers across the globe.

Global Alumni Network & Mentorship

Empowering the Next Generation of Civil Engineers

The strength of the ACI KUET Student Chapter lies in our lifelong relationship with Department of Civil Engineering alumni. From leading high-rise seismic design consultancies to pioneering sustainable cement chemistry and managing national megaprojects, our alumni actively mentor current students.

Structural Design & High-Rise

Senior Structural Design Director

Consultancy Lead • Arup / WSP / Thornton Tomasetti caliber

Specializing in performance-based seismic design, non-linear time-history response modeling, and concrete core wall shear systems for high-rise towers across Southeast Asia and North America. Mentors students on advanced ETABS modeling and structural lateral systems.

KUET Civil Engineering Alumnus
Materials Science & R&D

R&D & Quality Assurance Head

Cement & Ready-Mix Industry • LafargeHolcim / BSRM / Akij / Bashundhara

Directing formulation development for high-performance concrete, supplementary cementitious materials (micro-silica, GGBS, calcined clay), and low-carbon cement blends. Leads industrial plant tours and technical mix design masterclasses for chapter members.

KUET Civil Engineering Alumnus
Mega-Infrastructure Delivery

Senior Infrastructure Project Manager

Bridges & Mass Transit • Padma Bridge / Metro Rail / Expressway Projects

Managing construction supervision, pre-stressed concrete segmental girders, post-tensioned tendon grouting, and mass concrete temperature control in major national infrastructure projects. Provides site visit access and contract execution guidance.

KUET Civil Engineering Alumnus
Global Academia & PhD Research

Doctoral & Postdoctoral Fellow

Advanced Materials • UC Berkeley / UIUC / Purdue / NUS / TU Delft

Conducting breakthrough research on computational fracture mechanics, ultra-high-performance fiber-reinforced concrete (UHPFRC), and bio-mineralized self-healing concrete. Conducts 1-on-1 higher studies mentoring and research paper writing workshops.

KUET Civil Engineering Alumnus

Alumni Mentorship Offerings

Structured programs available exclusively for registered ACI KUET Student Chapter fellows

Higher Studies & PhD Mentorship

Personalized guidance on GRE/TOEFL preparation, contacting professors, tailoring Statements of Purpose (SOP), and securing funded Research Assistantships (RA) and Teaching Assistantships (TA) in top US/European universities.

Structural Industry Onboarding

Bridging the transition from undergraduate coursework to professional consultancy firms. Hands-on reviews of structural design calculation sheets, drawing detailing standards, and mock technical engineering interviews.

Research Co-Supervision & Papers

Collaborative opportunities for undergraduate thesis groups to publish in peer-reviewed journals (ACI Materials Journal, Construction and Building Materials) with co-authorship and technical editing support from overseas alumni.
