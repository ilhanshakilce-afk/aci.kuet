Event Overview • Truss Challenge 1.2

Pushing Structural Boundaries: From Balsa Craft to Catastrophic Failure

ReinforCE 1.2: Truss Challenge represented the hands-on fabrication milestone under the ReinforCE 1.0 intra series organized by the American Concrete Institute (ACI) Student Chapter at KUET. While Mechanics Mania tested abstract calculations, the Truss Challenge placed real physical materials into students' hands, forcing them to confront the unforgiving realities of timber grain direction, gusset joint eccentricities, out-of-plane lateral buckling, and glue shear strength.

Strict Geometric & Material Bounds

Span & Dimensions

Clear support span: 600 mm. Maximum bridge height: 160 mm. Minimum deck clearance: 80 mm for vehicular simulator transit.

Mass Limitation

Maximum bridge self-weight: 55 grams. Excess weight penalized exponentially on the structural efficiency calculation.

Allowable Materials

Uniform cross-section balsa wood strips (\(3\,\text{mm} \times 3\,\text{mm}\)) and standard cyanoacrylate / PVA wood adhesive. No composite reinforcement allowed.

Destructive Testing on the Hydraulic Floor

The competition culminated in a public testing session inside the KUET Department of Civil Engineering Concrete & Structures Laboratory. Each fabricated bridge was precisely weighed on a 0.01g electronic laboratory balance, rested upon calibrated steel knife-edge supports, and fitted with a central loading block.

A calibrated hydraulic load actuator applied monotonic downward displacement. Real-time load values were projected on live digital monitors as students and faculty watched tension members stretch, compression chords undergo Euler buckling, and joints explode at peak failure load (\(P_{\text{ult}}\)).

Official Scoring Formula
$$\text{Structural Efficiency} = \frac{\text{Ultimate Failure Load } (P_{\text{ult}} \text{ in kg})}{\text{Bridge Self-Mass } (M_{\text{self}} \text{ in kg})}$$

Top-performing structures achieved efficiency ratios exceeding 800× their own weight!

Faculty Mentorship & Safety Protocols

Supervised by departmental laboratory technical staff and Chapter Moderator Prof. Dr. Abu Zakir Morshed, the Truss Challenge served as an indispensable masterclass in structural detailing, teaching students that joint workmanship and symmetric alignment dictate real-world load survival far more than theoretical ideals.

Outcomes & Technical Legacy

  • Taught practical understanding of Euler buckling in compression chords vs brittle shear failures at wood joints.
  • Instilled laboratory safety practices, digital load calibration, and precision weighing protocols.
  • Prepared KUET students for dominance in the national-level Truss Trim segment of ReinforCE 1.0.
