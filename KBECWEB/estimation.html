ENGINEERING INTERCONNECTIVITY

How Civil Construction Works in Bangladesh: The 6-Phase Relational Pipeline

In professional Bangladesh civil engineering practice (taught at premier institutions like KUET, and governed by ACI 318 & BNBC 2020), every building project moves systematically through 6 interconnected phases. Each PDF document in this registry serves as a mandatory statutory or technical gear in this process.

Phase 01

Planning & Approval

Statutory Land Bylaws
RAJUK Imarat Bidhimala 2008/2025 • DAP 2016-2035 • Pourashabha 1996 • CDA • KDA

Sets Maximum Ground Coverage (MGC), Floor Area Ratio (FAR = 3.5), and mandatory setbacks (front, rear, side) based on plot size and access road width (min 20-25 ft).

Output Result
Max Gross Built-Up Sft
Explore 11 Bylaws →
Phase 02

Structural Analysis & Design

Codes & Specifications
BNBC 2020 (Parts 6 & 8) • ACI 318-19 • PWD Standard Specifications

Calculates dead, live, wind, and seismic loads (1.2D + 1.6L). Specifies concrete strength ($f'_c = 3,000$ psi), rebar yield strength ($f_y = 72,500$ psi), and aggregate sieve sizes.

Output Result
Concrete Cft & Rebar Tons
View 6 Standards →
Phase 03

Rate Analysis & BOQ

Government Price Schedules
PWD Civil SoR 2022 (Ch 01-33) • PWD E/M (Subhead 01-21) • LGED • RHD

Applies official Government gazetted unit rates for earthwork, RCC casting, brick masonry, tiles, doors, plumbing, electrical sub-station, and elevators.

Output Result
Itemized BOQ & Cost
Browse 72 Schedules →
Phase 04

Statutory Clearances

Life Safety & Environment
Fire Prevention Act 2003 / Rules 2014 • DoE ECR 2023 • Air Pollution Rules 2022

Mandatory Fire NOC for >6 storeys (>20m), fire hydrants, wet risers, DoE environmental clearance (ECC), sewage treatment plants (STP/ETP), and scaffolding dust screens.

Output Result
NOC & Safety Additions
Review 6 Clearances →
Phase 05

Procurement & Tendering

Contracts & Legal Framework
Public Procurement Act 2006 (PPA) • Public Procurement Rules 2008 (PPR)

Governs e-GP bidding, tender evaluation committees (TEC), 10% performance security guarantees, variation orders (max 15%), and liquidated damages formulas.

Output Result
Enforceable Contract
