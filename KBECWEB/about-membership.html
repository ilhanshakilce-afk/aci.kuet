Why Join ACI KUET?

Accelerate your structural engineering journey with free global ACI International membership, laboratory casting privileges, national competition dominance, and elite industry networking.

Global Student Membership Advantage

Bridging Classroom Mechanics with World-Class Engineering Practice

As an undergraduate in the Department of Civil Engineering at KUET, joining the American Concrete Institute (ACI) Student Chapter transforms theoretical knowledge into competitive engineering mastery. From international travel grants to structural software mastery, explore how chapter membership elevates your career trajectory.

Free Global ACI USA Membership

Gain complimentary student membership to ACI International (Farmington Hills, Michigan, USA), valued at $250/year. Access over 10,000+ peer-reviewed ACI journal papers, technical committee documents, and digital code manuals.

International & National Competitions

Represent KUET at premier competitions including the ACI Concrete Egg Protection Device, Fiber-Reinforced Concrete Bowling Ball Competition, and National ReinforCE Fest. Opportunity to qualify for USA convention travel sponsorships.

Hands-on Concrete Testing in KUET Lab

Conduct real mix design casting, compressive strength crushing on the 2000 kN Universal Testing Machine (UTM), and ultrasonic pulse velocity scans in KUET’s state-of-the-art Departmental Concrete Lab.

Structural Software Mastery Bootcamps

Receive hands-on, project-based training on industry-standard engineering software including CSI ETABS 21, CSI SAP2000, SAFE foundation detailing, and AutoCAD structural drafting led by senior fellows.

Alumni & Industry Mentorship Network

Direct access to senior KUET civil engineering alumni practicing across top structural consultancies (Arup, WSP, Thornton Tomasetti) and leading cement & steel industries (BSRM, LafargeHolcim, Akij, Bashundhara).

Cryptographically Verified Credentials

Earn official digital certificates of participation, leadership tenure, and competition standing, verifiable via the official ACI KUET verification engine (acikuet.org/verify) for global graduate school admissions.

How Chapter Induction Works

Simple 4-stage onboarding pathway for undergraduate students of Civil Engineering, KUET

1

Online Application

Fill out the official chapter registration form below with your KUET student roll and academic email.

2

Department Verification

Academic cohort credentials verified by the ACI KUET Secretariat under the Department of Civil Engineering.

3

ACI USA Portal Access

Receive your unique international ACI Member ID giving immediate free access to concrete.org technical libraries.

4

Team Induction

Assigned to technical sub-committees (Lab Casting, Competition Teams, Media/Design, or Logistics & Events).
