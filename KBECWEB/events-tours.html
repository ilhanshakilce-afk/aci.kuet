Regional Khulna Industrial Field Trips & Mega-Sites

Field exposure is central to concrete education. Prioritizing high-value structural installations situated within the Khulna metropolitan and regional corridor.

Industrial Immersion

Eight Core Technical Site Expeditions

Guided site walkthroughs connecting lecture room theories to heavy structural execution in Bangladesh.

Site 01 | Rupsha, Khulna

Seven Rings Cement Grinding Plant

Modern Vertical Roller Mill (VRM) grinding kinetics, automated clinker-slag blending, computerized chemical QA laboratories, and automated packaging lines.

Site 02 | Noapara Corridor

Akij Cement Factory

High-capacity clinker storage silos, ball mill grinding systems, blended cement production protocols, and automated bulk loading terminals.

Site 03 | Khulna Metro

Bashundhara Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) Plant

Automated aggregate batching bins, twin-shaft pan mixers, transit mixer fleet dispatching, on-site slump flow testing, and concrete test cylinder curing.

Site 04 | Regional Facility

Mir Concrete Products Precast Yard

Industrial manufacturing of precast reinforced concrete pipes, interlocking pavers, hollow concrete blocks, prestressed bridge girders, and high-pressure steam curing chambers.

Site 05 | Khulna

Khan Jahan Ali Rupsha Bridge

Prestressed concrete box girder structural inspection, elastomeric bearing pads, seismic expansion joints, river training revetments, and substructure pier health monitoring.

Site 06 | KSY Khulna

Khulna Shipyard (KSY) Heavy Dry Dock

Heavy civil dry dock construction, massive reinforced concrete retaining walls, quay wall sheet piling, steel-concrete marine connections, and dock gate hydraulic mechanisms.

Site 07 | Mongla Port

Mongla Coastal Infrastructure & Jetties

Durable marine concrete in aggressive coastal saline environments, sulfate-resistant slag matrices, concrete armor blocks (CC blocks), and coastal revetment geotextile tubes.

Site 08 | Padma Rail Link

Rupsha Rail Bridge (Padma Rail Link)

High-capacity long-span railway bridge, deep bored pile integrity testing (Cross-Hole Sonic Logging and Osterberg cell load tests), and high-performance concrete mix placement.

ACI KUET Student Chapter

Rooted in the Department of Civil Engineering at KUET and chartered by the American Concrete Institute (ACI), the ACI KUET Student Chapter is a dynamic platform for aspiring civil and materials engineers. We strive to cultivate hands-on innovation, sustainable construction practices, and leadership, connecting KUET students with nationwide industry networks and global engineering forums.
