Architecting Your First Complete 3D Building Model from Scratch

When approaching computational structural modeling for the first time, complex coordinate systems and menus can feel daunting. This structured milestone roadmap breaks down every step into intuitive civil engineering logic.

Step 01 • System Setup

Unit Configuration & Architectural Grid Definition

Upon launching ETABS, first verify Display Units (US Customary or Metric SI). Define exact column coordinate intersections and vertical story levels:

  • Define Base to Plinth Level (GL to PL: 4 to 6 ft) as the foundation tie-beam elevation
  • Set typical residential floor story heights (10 ft or 3.048 m)
  • Activate Master Story and Similar Story editing modes to streamline repetitive floor drafting
KUET Senior Tip: Never round off architectural grid dimensions casually. Always cross-check centerline offsets with architectural AutoCAD drawings before placing structural columns.
Step 02 • Materials

Material Property Definition: Concrete & Rebar Grades

Structural capacity is governed by specified materials. Define characteristic compressive strength and steel yield properties under Define > Material Properties:

  • Concrete Grade: 20 MPa / 25 MPa / 4000 psi (Modulus of Elasticity: Ec = 4700√f'c MPa per ACI 318)
  • Rebar Steel: ASTM A615 Grade 60 or BDS ISO 6935-2 500W (Fy = 500 MPa / 72.5 ksi) for main longitudinal reinforcement and ties
  • Verify Mass Density (Plain Concrete: 24 kN/m³, Reinforced Concrete: 25 kN/m³ / 150 pcf)
Step 03 • Frame Modeling

Structural Modeling: Columns, Beams & Floor Slabs

Begin with initial preliminary structural cross-sections based on standard span-to-depth ratios:

  • Place column sections at grid intersections using the Quick Draw Columns tool
  • Verify column orientations and principal bending axes to align with architectural room clearances and lateral frame resistance
  • Connect column-to-column perimeter and interior frame beams using Quick Draw Beams
  • Model slab panels across each enclosed bay using the Draw Floor/Wall tool (Membrane vs Shell-Thin)
Step 04 • Load Assignment

Gravity Load Application: Dead, Live, Floor Finish & Partition Walls

ETABS automatically computes concrete member self-weights based on material density. Superimposed dead and live loads must be applied manually per BNBC 2020 Part 6:

  • Floor Finish (FF): 25 to 30 psf (1.2 to 1.5 kN/m²) uniform dead load on slabs
  • Live Load (LL): 42 psf (2.0 kN/m²) for residential occupancy, 63 psf (3.0 kN/m²) for commercial office spaces
  • Partition Wall (PW): 5-inch brick masonry wall line loads (0.45 to 0.55 kip/ft) applied directly on beams supporting walls
Step 05 • Quality Audit

Geometric Integrity Audit & Analysis Execution

  • Navigate to Analyze > Check Model to verify zero element overlaps and joint alignment tolerance

    • Assign Fixed (or Pinned) base restraints to all bottom support joints (Assign > Joint > Restraints)
    • Execute finite element solution by clicking Run Analysis (or pressing F5)
    • Inspect the animated 3D Deformed Shape to verify overall structural equilibrium and joint continuity
    Common Beginner Pitfall: If an animated frame shows disconnected beams flying off, your joints did not snap properly to grid intersections. Re-align joint coordinates before reading bending moments!

    Senior Structural Consultancy Level

    Lecture Overview: Comprehensive procedure for BNBC 2020 Equivalent Static, Response Spectrum scaling, Diaphragm assignment, and P-Delta stability analysis.

    Core Workflows of Professional Structural Consultancy Engineers

    Structural engineering extends far beyond gravity frame modeling. Real-world high-rise towers in Bangladesh must be rigorously designed against cyclone winds, seismic ground motions, and non-linear second-order lateral drift:

    Pro Step 01 • Code Formulation

    Rigid Diaphragm Modeling & Lateral Mass Source Definition

    To ensure floor slabs distribute horizontal seismic and wind forces across resisting lateral vertical elements like a monolithic rigid plate:

    • Assign Rigid Diaphragms (Assign > Shell > Diaphragms > D1) across every floor slab plane
    • Define Mass Source: 1.0 Dead Load + 1.0 Floor Finish + 1.0 Partition Wall + 0.25 Live Load (per BNBC 2020 Sec 2.5)
    • Compute Center of Mass (CM) and Center of Rigidity (CR) eccentricity offsets (5% accidental torsion)
    Pro Step 02 • Lateral Loads

    BNBC 2020 Wind Tunnel Parameters & Response Spectrum Functions

    Assign windward and leeward pressures alongside spectral accelerations based on local geotechnical and tectonic zone coefficients:

    • Wind Load (BNBC 2020): Basic Wind Speed V = 63 m/s (Khulna), Exposure Category B or C, Gust Effect Factor G = 0.85
    • Seismic Zone: Khulna Zone 2 (Z = 0.20), Soil Site Class SD/SE, Structural System SMRF (R = 8.0, I = 1.0 or 1.25)
    • Response Spectrum Function: Construct BNBC 2020 design spectrum curve and scale dynamic base shear to at least 100% of equivalent static base shear
    Pro Step 03 • Stiffness Modifiers

    P-Delta Geometric Non-Linearity & Cracked Section Property Modifiers

    During lateral seismic excursions, micro-cracks form in concrete, reducing effective cross-sectional stiffness. ACI 318 and BNBC mandate moment of inertia modifiers:

    • Column Moment of Inertia Modifier: I22 = 0.70, I33 = 0.70
    • Beam Moment of Inertia Modifier: I22 = 0.35, I33 = 0.35
    • Slab Membrane/Bending Modifiers: f11 = f22 = m11 = m22 = 0.25
    • Activate P-Delta Second-Order Analysis: Iterative Based on Loads (1.2 DL + 0.5 LL)
    Pro Step 04 • Serviceability & Drift

    Story Drift Limits & Torsional Irregularity Audit

    Beyond strength, lateral drift must be strictly constrained to prevent cladding failure, glass breakage, and human discomfort:

    • Maximum Inelastic Story Drift: Δm = 0.7 · R · Δs ≤ 0.04 hsx (BNBC 2020 limit)
    • Torsional Irregularity Ratio: Δmax / Δavg ≤ 1.20 (Values exceeding 1.40 indicate extreme torsional irregularity requiring structural shear wall reconfiguration)
    • Soft Story / Weak Story Diagnostics (Verify lower story lateral stiffness is not less than 70% of upper floor stiffness)
    Pro Step 05 • Member Design

    Reinforced Concrete Frame Design & Longitudinal Steel Optimization

    Initiating Design > Concrete Frame Design > Start Design Check generates required longitudinal rebar area (Ast) across all critical sections:

    • Column Reinforcement Ratio: 1.0% ≤ ρ ≤ 4.0% (Never exceed 4% in practice to prevent concrete placement rebar congestion)
    • Beam flexural steel detailing: Top negative steel over supports and bottom positive steel at mid-span with seismic splice confinement
    • Shear wall Pier and Spandrel reinforcement detailing with special boundary elements where compressive strains exceed 0.002
    Consultancy Interview Pro-Tip: If a column fails in an engineering test, interviewers will ask: "Do you increase concrete grade or column dimensions?" In tall buildings, increasing column depth along the weak axis is exponentially more effective due to the cubic inertia term (b·h³/12)!
