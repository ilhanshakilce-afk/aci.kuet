Awards, Honors & Global Recognition Roadmap

Dual-track award roadmap: Establishing KUET's inter-university leadership across Bangladesh and pursuing the global ACI SYPAC "Outstanding Student Chapter" award.

National Honor

Concase Structural Case Champions

Podium recognition for advanced forensic concrete structural damage modeling and composite fiber retrofitting solutions presented by KUET civil engineering teams.

International Track

Global ACI SYPAC Outstanding Chapter Pathway

Annual reporting compliance under ACI International Headquarters scoring rubric: qualifying through certified bootcamps, faculty advisor engagement, and community civic builds.

Technical Competency

150+ Certified Software Engineers

Annual milestone verifying that over 150 undergraduate students achieve certified competency across AutoCAD, ETABS, SAFE, Revit, and Civil 3D.

Community Service

Sustainable Civic Concrete Build

Philanthropic civic engineering initiatives casting eco-friendly fly-ash concrete walkways and community sanitation slabs in rural Khulna communities.
