Technical Events & Hands-On Engineering Workshops

Bridging theoretical mechanics with real lab experimentation in the KUET Concrete Testing Laboratory and Civil Engineering Computing Center.

Laboratory Immersion

Core Technical Programs

Concrete Mix & Casting Trials

Practical casting sessions exploring ACI 211.1 mix proportioning, water-cement ratios, slump flow consistency, and cube/cylinder casting for 7, 14, and 28-day UTM crushing.

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Masterclass

Hands-on operational training with Rebound Hammer (Schmidt Hammer) and Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) testers for in-situ structural health diagnostics.

ETABS & SAP2000 Structural Hackathon

Multi-story building modeling, seismic dynamic response analysis, lateral wind load distribution per BNBC 2020, and shear wall design workshops.

CSI SAFE Foundation Engineering

Modeling isolated footings, mat foundations, post-tensioned slabs, soil spring subgrade reactions, and punching shear verification.

Sustainable Concrete & Pozzolanic Binders

Industrial fly ash, slag cement, and silica fume replacement trials evaluating heat of hydration reduction and chloride resistance in coastal saline environments.

Ready-Mix Batching & Quality Control

Technical sessions on automated batching plant economics, chemical superplasticizers/retarders, and transit mixer dispatch logistics in collaboration with industry partners.

ACI KUET Student Chapter

Rooted in the Department of Civil Engineering at KUET and chartered by the American Concrete Institute (ACI), the ACI KUET Student Chapter is a dynamic platform for aspiring civil and materials engineers. We strive to cultivate hands-on innovation, sustainable construction practices, and leadership, connecting KUET students with nationwide industry networks and global engineering forums.
